## What changed (the mechanism)

  Source: National Mortgage News, "End of FHA pandemic relief to kick off wave of foreclosures," https://www.nationalmortgagenews.com/news/end-of-fha-pandemic-relief-to-kick-off-wave-of-foreclosures — retrieved 2026-07-31.
