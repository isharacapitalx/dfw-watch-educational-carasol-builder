Everyone assumes foreclosures died with 2008. A quiet rule change just made Texas the #1 state in the country for them. 👀

📌 A new 3-month trial payment period is now required (as of February 2026) before a permanent modification gets approved
📌 Texas led all 50 states with 10,617 foreclosure starts in Q1 2026 — more than any other state, including Florida
📌 Dallas alone saw 2,427 foreclosure starts that same quarter
📌 Nationally, banks repossessed 14,020 homes last quarter, up 45% year over year — inventory that's starting to show up in listings
