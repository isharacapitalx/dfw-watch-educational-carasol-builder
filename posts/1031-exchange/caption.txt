The rule that turns one rental into a $2M portfolio — tax deferred. 🏡

Most owners hand the IRS a check every time they sell. Wealthy investors almost never do — they use Section 1031 of the tax code, and it's open to anyone.

📌 The rule: sell an investment property, roll the full proceeds into another like-kind investment property, and every dollar of capital gains tax is deferred.

📌 The catch: 45 days to identify the replacement property, 180 days to close. Miss either window and the deferral is gone.

📌 The play: buy, hold, exchange up, repeat — your equity compounds with money the IRS hasn't touched yet.
